# Rules for system-design/

## Content Restrictions
- **No interview mentions** — no «собеседование», «интервью», «подготовка к интервью». Notes are technical material, not cheat sheets.
- **No author/location references** — no personal info, no Казахстан, Алматы, etc.
- **Classical CS examples** — cities: San Francisco, New York, London, Tokyo. Companies: Amazon, Netflix, Twitter/X, Google. People: Alice, Bob, Charlie.
